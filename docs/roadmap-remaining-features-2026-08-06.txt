ORIGINAL REMAINING SCOPE (completed 2026-08-08):

  5. Loyalty / referral program
     - Points or credit earned per order, redeemable like a gift card
       (natural reuse of the gift_cards ledger pattern from Feature 4).
     - Referral codes: existing customer refers a new one, both get a
       reward once the referred customer's first order ships.
     - Needs its own admin on/off switch (commerce.loyalty.enabled), same
       10-step feature-toggle checklist used for Features 3 and 4.

  6. Local pickup points
     - Alternative to home delivery: customer selects a pickup location at
       checkout instead of paying a delivery fee.
     - Needs a `pickup_points` table (name, address, hours) an admin
       manages, a checkout-time selector, and order fulfilment changes
       (delivery_status semantics for a pickup order vs a shipped one).
     - Needs its own admin on/off switch.

  7. Seasonal pre-order / harvest calendar
     - Customers reserve a not-yet-harvested product ahead of its expected
       harvest window; charged (or authorized) at order time, fulfilled
       when the harvest actually comes in.
     - Touches inventory (a pre-order reserves against a future harvest,
       not current on_hand stock) and needs an admin harvest-calendar
       editor (expected windows per product).
     - Needs its own admin on/off switch.

  8. Delivery logistics (slots/zones)
     - Real delivery slot selection (day + time window) at checkout, and
       serviceable-zone enforcement (some postal codes/regions cannot be
       delivered to, or have different fees/lead times).
     - Bigger lift than the others: touches checkout validation, the
       delivery-fee calculation already in services/checkout.py, and
       needs an admin zones/slots management page.
     - Needs its own admin on/off switch.

  9. B2B / bulk ordering
     - A wholesale/bulk pricing tier for business customers -- quantity
       price breaks, a separate account type or flag, possibly invoicing
       instead of pay-at-checkout.
     - Largest unknown of the remaining five -- touches pricing,
       potentially the customer/account model, and possibly a different
       checkout flow entirely (net-terms invoicing vs immediate payment).
     - Needs its own admin on/off switch.

Suggested build order if/when resumed: 5 and 6 are the most self-contained
and closest in shape to what already exists (5 reuses the gift-card ledger
pattern directly; 6 is a small new table + a checkout selector, similar
scope to Feature 4). 7 and 8 both touch checkout/inventory more deeply and
should come after those two are proven out. 9 is the least well-specified
and the biggest architectural unknown -- worth a dedicated scoping
conversation with the user before starting, not just a research pass.
